# Omnis — Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Unreal project configuration, C++ module, Cycle, Nexo, progression and save foundations.

## Phase 2 — Vertical slice

Playable character, exploration, first NPC interaction, decision state, combat, cycle end and Nexo loop.

## Phase 3 — World simulation

World state, resources, traversal, environmental simulation and NPC schedules.

## Phase 4 — Narrative and systemic expansion

Factions, quests, consequences, cinematics, reputation and systemic AI.

## Phase 5 — Online foundation

Authentication, sessions, authoritative persistence and controlled multiplayer prototypes.

## Phase 6 — Persistent world

Cities, territory, sieges, economy and large-scale online operations, driven by measured capacity and actual service requirements.
