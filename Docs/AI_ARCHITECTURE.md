# Omnis — AI Architecture

AI will be divided into perception, behavior, navigation, NPC state and a higher-level director.

## NPC lifecycle

An NPC combines identity, routine, relationships, goals and current world context. Schedules can create emergent encounters that become opportunities for narrative or gameplay.

## Director

The director may influence encounter pacing and systemic pressure, but it must not silently overwrite authoritative quest or progression state.
